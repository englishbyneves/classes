What Would You Do?

★ Level B1 ⏱ 50 min 🤔 Hypotheticals & Dilemmas
By the end of today, you can talk about imaginary situations and tough choices using "If I… I would…" without stopping to translate.
Teacher reference — plano & meta (toque para abrir)

Código: B1-CONV-03 · Módulo: Conversation Block · Tema: Hypotheticals & dilemmas

Objetivo comunicativo: falar sobre situações imaginárias e escolhas difíceis; justificar decisões; reagir a dilemas de outras pessoas.

Gramática em contexto (não ensinar isolada): second conditional (If I won…, I'd…), would/wouldn't, would rather, might/probably pra suavizar. Introduzir via chunk, não via regra.

Correção: recast do "would" e da forma do verbo (If I had… não "If I have"). Corrige no fim pra não travar o fluxo.

Critério de sucesso: o aluno sustenta uma decisão hipotética por 60s, usa "I'd / I wouldn't" corretamente 3+ vezes e lida com um "plot twist" sem recorrer ao português.

Homework: DuoCards (chunks de 2nd conditional) + áudio de 60s "If I could change one thing about my routine…".

Round 1 · Warm-up

Quick choices

Snap decision — pick one out loud and say why in a few words. No "I don't know"! 😄

  • 1

    Teleport anywhere right now — beach or big city?

  • 2

    Free superpower: fly or be invisible?

  • 3

    Win R$1,000 today or R$10,000 in a year?

  • 4

    Never use your phone for a week or never eat your favourite food for a month?

Round 2 · Guided

If I could…

Pick a card and answer with "If I…, I'd…" — then keep going for 30 seconds. Add why and what would happen next.

…won the lottery …could live in any country …could have any job …could meet anyone in the world …could master one skill instantly …could be invisible for one day
Stretch it: pick one and talk for a full 60 seconds. What else would change in your life?
Round 3 · Decide

Tough calls

Read each dilemma aloud and decide. Say what you'd do and defend it. There's no "right" answer — only how well you explain it.

  • A

    You find a wallet with R$500 and no ID, only a photo. What would you do?

  • B

    Your best friend asks you to lie to cover for them. Would you do it?

  • C

    You're offered your dream job — but in another country, starting next month. Do you go?

  • D

    A free trip anywhere, but you have to leave tomorrow and tell no one. Would you take it?

  • E

    You could know the date of one future event. Would you want to?
