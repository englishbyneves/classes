Let's Eat!

★ Level A2 ⏱ 50 min 🍽️ Food & ordering
By the end of today, you can talk about food you like, describe a meal, and order in a café or restaurant in English.
Teacher reference — plano & meta (toque para abrir)

Código: A2-LIFE-02 · Módulo: Everyday Life · Tema: Food, preferences & ordering out

Objetivo comunicativo: dizer o que gosta/não gosta de comer; descrever um prato simples; pedir comida/bebida num café ou restaurante com naturalidade.

Gramática em contexto (não ensinar isolada, usar via frase-modelo): like/love/don't like + -ing ou substantivo, would like / I'll have pra pedir, Can I…? / Could I…?, there is/are no cardápio.

Apoio A2: mini-diálogo modelo visível para o role-play, frases-modelo, muito exemplo pronto. Correção por recast; deixa o aluno terminar a fala antes de corrigir.

Critério de sucesso: o aluno faz um pedido completo (bebida + comida + pergunta) no role-play, diz 2 coisas que gosta/não gosta e descreve 1 prato por ~30s.

Homework: DuoCards (chunks da aula) + áudio de 30s "My favourite meal is…".

Round 2 · Guided

My food favourites

Finish each sentence out loud. Then say why, or when you eat it.

I really love .
I don't like very much.
On a special day, I eat .
I could eat every day!
Keep going for 30 seconds: describe your favourite meal. What's in it? Who makes it? Use the timer, bottom right.
Round 3 · Model

At the café

Read this little dialogue out loud with your teacher. Then swap roles and change the order to what YOU want.

Staff
Hi! What can I get you?
You
Hi! Can I have a cappuccino, please?
Staff
Sure. Anything to eat?
You
Yes, I'll have a slice of cake. How much is it?
Staff
That's £5.50. For here or to go?
You
For here, please. Thank you!

After class

Homework

  • DuoCards: add today's phrases (I'll have…, Can I have…?, I'm not a fan of…) and review 2×.
  • Record a 30-second voice note: "My favourite meal is…" — say what's in it and when you eat it.
  • Real mission: next time you order a coffee or food, say the order in English in your head first. 😉
