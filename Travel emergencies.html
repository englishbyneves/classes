Quick reference (only if needed)
"I lost my…" / "My bag didn't arrive" are ready phrases — no past tense lesson needed.
I have a + problem: I have a headache / a fever / a cough.
My + part + hurts: My back hurts. My arm hurts.
Describe: It's a + size + color + thing (+ with…): It's a small red backpack with stickers.
