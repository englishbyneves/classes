You walk into the Ocean View Hotel. You have a reservation for 3 nights, one person. You want a room with a view if possible, and you need the Wi-Fi password. Do your part — out loud.

one natural version

"Hi, good evening. I have a reservation. My name is Thiago Ritz. It's for three nights, one person. Here's my passport. Could I have a room with a view, please? And can I get the Wi-Fi password?"

ROLE-PLAYNow the whole thing: from "Good evening" to walking away with your key, your room number, and the Wi-Fi info.

You go into room 314. The AC is too cold, the TV is not working, and you need two more towels. Call the front desk and solve all three problems in one call. Out loud.

one natural version

"Hi, this is room 314. I have some problems. First, the AC is very cold — can you help me turn it down? Also, the TV is not working. And can I get two more towels, please? Thank you."

ROLE-PLAYNow the whole thing: pick 4 problems from the vocab cards. Call the front desk. Describe each one clearly, one by one.

It's 8 pm and you're hungry. Go to reception. Ask for a cheap and good place to eat, ask if it's safe to walk there, and ask if the receptionist has any recommendation for tomorrow. Answer her small talk too.

one natural version

"Hi, quick question. Where can I eat something good and cheap near here? Is it safe to walk there at night?"

"First time in Miami?" — "Yes, first time! I'm going to Orlando next week."

"Great. Do you have any recommendation for tomorrow — the beach, something to do?"

ROLE-PLAYNow the whole thing: from arriving at the desk to walking out with a plan for dinner and tomorrow.

It's your last day. You're checking out at 11 am but your flight to Orlando is at 7 pm. You want to leave your bags at the hotel and get one last tip for lunch nearby.

one natural version

Plan your Orlando hotel

Answer out loud, in full sentences, using the words from today.

  • Where is your hotel in Orlando? Near Disney or downtown?
  • How many nights are you staying?
  • Do you want a room with a view? Of what?
