Warm up — speak now

No reading first. Just talk. Short answers are fine.

  • Have you ever eaten at Five Guys? If yes — what did you order? If no — what's your favourite burger place?Você já comeu no Five Guys? O que pediu?
  • Look at the photo. What can you see? Describe it in English for 30 seconds.Olhe a foto. O que você vê? Descreva por 30 segundos.
  • When you eat out, do you prefer to eat in or take it away?Você prefere comer no local ou levar?
  • On your next trip, what food do you want to try?Na sua próxima viagem, que comida quer provar?
💡 You don't need perfect English. You need to be understood. That's the whole goal today.

Challenge 1
Order for two people. Your friend wants a veggie sandwich and a strawberry milkshake.
Challenge 2 — fix a problem
You got onions, but you said no onions. Fix it politely: "Sorry, I think there's a mix-up — I asked for no onions."
Challenge 3 — ask a question
Ask the cashier: "Are the Cajun fries spicy?" or "Can I get some water?"

Grammar reference (optional)
Polite requests for ordering, kept in context — not a grammar lesson:

• Can I get…? / Can I have…? / I'll have… — all mean "I want this, please". Most natural at a counter: Can I get a cheeseburger, please?
• Removing something: without or no + topping — without onions / no onions.
• Adding more: extra — extra cheese.
• UK vs US: to take away (UK) = to go (US); toilet (UK) = restroom (US).
