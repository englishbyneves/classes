How to say it

Grammar reference
• my / your / his / her → my mother (your family), her brother (a woman's), his sister (a man's).
• have / has → I have two sisters. She has one brother.
• 's (possession) → my mother's name = the name of my mother.
• How old → How old is he? He's 20.
