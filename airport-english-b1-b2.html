You're at the desk. You have one suitcase to check in and a backpack to carry on. You'd like a window seat, and you want to know if the flight is on time. Do your part — out loud.

one natural version

"Hi, I'm checking in for the flight to Lisbon. Just one bag to check in — this is my hand luggage. Could I have a window seat, please? And is the flight on time?"

ROLE-PLAYNow the whole thing: from "Good morning" to walking away with your boarding pass, knowing your gate and boarding time.

5Your turn — the interviewdo the whole thingoutput

You hand over your passport. Answer the officer's questions one by one — short and confident, no hesitation. You're on holiday for two weeks, staying with family, flying home on the 30th.

a confident exchange

"Purpose of your visit?" — "I'm here on holiday, visiting family."

"How long are you staying?" — "Two weeks."

"Where are you staying?" — "With my cousin, in the city centre. I've got the address here."

"Do you have a return ticket?" — "Yes, I fly back on the 30th — I can show you the booking."

ROLE-PLAYNow do it for real: the full passport-control conversation, start to "Enjoy your stay." Keep every answer short and steady.

one natural version

"Hi, I think my bag is missing. I was on the flight from Lisbon, BA245. It's a medium black suitcase with a red ribbon. I'm staying at the Centro Hotel — here's my baggage tag."
