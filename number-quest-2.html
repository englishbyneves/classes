Level 3~5 min

The tens: 30 → 100

Read each one out loud. Watch the spelling traps. Tap a card for a real-life use.

Spelling traps: it's forty (not "fourty"), fifty, and ninety — easy to get wrong when you write them.
Level 4~5 min

In between: 21 → 99

Just glue two words with a hyphen: twenty + one → twenty-one. Tap for examples.

Level 5~5 min

Hundreds: 100 → 999

One word: hundred (never "hundreds" for a number). Tap a card to hear the pattern.

British tip (that's us! 🇬🇧): we put "and" before the last part — one hundred and twenty-five, not "one hundred twenty-five". Americans drop the "and". Both are understood.
Level 6~5 min

Thousands: 1,000 → 10,000

Same idea, bigger word: thousand. Tap for real prices and salaries.

Two ways for round numbers: 1,500 can be "one thousand five hundred" or, casually, "fifteen hundred". Both are correct.
