🎤 Your turn
Tell the whole story
Choose your best memory from today and tell it from start to finish. No reading.
1
Start with when and where: "Last year, I was in…"
2
Say what you did, in order: "First… then… after that…"
3
Finish with how you felt: "It was… I felt…"
