5

British small talk

In the UK, the weather is how people start a chat. Tap each card.

🇬🇧 Golden rule: you don't need real information. Someone says "Cold today, isn't it?" You just agree: "Yeah, freezing!" 😄
Grammar reference
• Now → It's + verb-ing: It's raining. It's snowing.
• A fact → It's + adjective: It's sunny. It's windy. It's 10 degrees.
• Forecast → It's going to + verb: It's going to rain later.
• Tag question → …, isn't it? Lovely day, isn't it?
