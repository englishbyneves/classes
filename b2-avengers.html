Today is all about talking. You'll argue a case, disagree politely, and defend your opinion on the big questions the Avengers raise.
01Round One

Warm Up

Quickfire. Say the first thing that comes to mind, then back it up.

  • Are you into superhero films, or are you tired of them? Be honest.
  • Who's the greatest superhero of all time, and why?
  • Would you actually want superpowers, or is normal life better?
  • Can you think of a villain who kind of had a point?

Talking Points

03Combat Training

How to Argue

The phrases that keep a debate flowing. Say each one out loud.

🔑 The strongest move in English debate: concede, then counter. "That may be true, but..." You sound fair and still win the point.
04Main Event

The Debate Arena

Read the motion. Tap your side. Use the openers, answer the counter-attack, and hold the floor for 60 seconds.

05Deep Dive

The Big Questions

No right answers. Take your time, think out loud, and change your mind if you want.

06Civil War

Pick a Side

The classic split. Security or freedom? Choose your team and defend it.

Team Cap

Heroes must stay free to act on their conscience.

Governments can't be trusted with that power.

Freedom first
VS

Team Iron Man

Your mission

  1. Choose the side you disagree with. Harder, more useful.
  2. Prepare three solid arguments for it.
  3. Debate for three minutes. No switching sides!
07Final Round

Back to Reality

Forget the capes. Pick a card and talk for a full minute.

Grammar reference
• 3rd conditional (unreal past) → If Tony hadn't done it, everyone would have died.
• Modals of deduction → He must have known. She can't have survived. They might have escaped.
• Concession → Although / Even though he's a villain, he has a point. Despite his crimes, people like him.
• Softening opinions → I'd argue that... It could be said that...
