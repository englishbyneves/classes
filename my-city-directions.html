🗺️ Map challenge
Explore the city
Look at the map, then answer the questions.
Where is the pharmacy?

What is opposite the bakery?

🎭 Role-play
A tourist in town
A tourist needs help. Fill in the local's answers, then act it out and swap roles.
Tourist
Excuse me! I'm new in town. Is there a supermarket near here?
You (local)
Yes, there is.
Tourist
Is it far?
You (local)
Tourist
Thank you! And how can I get to the park?
You (local)

📝 Writing
My neighbourhood
Write 60 to 80 words about the area where you live.
I live in a neighbourhood called…Near my house, there is…There are many…My favourite place is…I like it because…
⭐
🏆 Final project
My dream neighbourhood
Put the three lessons together. Build your ideal neighbourhood with your dream home inside it.
1My house. Draw a home with at least five rooms.
2My furniture. Choose ten pieces and say where they are.
3My neighbourhood. Add eight places around your home.
4Directions. Explain how to get from your house to three places.
5Writing. Write 80 to 120 words about your dream neighbourhood.
6Present. Show your project and talk about it out loud.
