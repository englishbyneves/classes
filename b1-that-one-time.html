That One Time…

★ Level B1 ⏱ 50 min 📖 Storytelling
By the end of today, you can tell a short story with a clear start, middle and end — and react naturally when someone tells you theirs.
Teacher reference — plano & meta (toque para abrir)

Código: B1-CONV-02 · Módulo: Conversation Block · Tema: Telling & reacting to stories

Objetivo comunicativo: narrar um acontecimento pessoal com começo/meio/fim; reagir a histórias; fazer perguntas de follow-up pra manter a conversa viva.

Gramática em contexto (não ensinar isolada): past simple vs past continuous (I was walking when…), narrative connectors (then, suddenly, in the end, so), time markers (last year, a few weeks ago).

Correção: recast dos tempos verbais; devolve 2–3 frases reformuladas no fim. Deixa o aluno terminar a história antes de corrigir.

Critério de sucesso: o aluno conta uma história de ~90s com conectores, reage à história do professor e faz pelo menos 1 pergunta de follow-up.

Homework: DuoCards (connectors + chunks) + áudio de 90s "The best/worst trip I've had".

Round 2 · Guided

Finish the story

Read the opening out loud, then keep it going for 30 seconds. Use at least one "sequence" chunk. (Timer, bottom right.)

A. "Last summer, I was travelling alone for the first time. Everything was fine… until I arrived and realised…"
B. "It was a normal Tuesday. I was just scrolling on my phone when suddenly my friend called me and said…"
C. "I'll never forget my first day at that job. I walked in, and the first thing that happened was…"
Round 3 · React

Don't just nod — react!

Your teacher tells you a few mini-stories. After each one, you have to do two things: react with a chunk and ask one follow-up question. Silence is not allowed. 😄

Your job every time: React ("No way!" / "That's crazy!") → then ask ("So what did you do?" / "How did you feel?").
