My Daily Routine

What do you do every day? Today you learn the words to talk about your day — from the moment you wake up to the moment you go to bed.

1I can say what I do every day.
2I can say what time I do it.
3I can tell my day in order.
4I can ask about someone's routine.

Quick reference (only if needed)
I + action: I wake up · I have breakfast · I go to work.
Time: at 7:00 · at 7:30 (seven thirty) · at noon · at midnight.
Part of the day: in the morning · in the afternoon · in the evening · at night.
Order: First, … Then … After that, … Finally, …
