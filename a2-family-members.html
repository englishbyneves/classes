Talk about the people in your life
By the end of today, you can introduce a family member, say what they do and what they're like, and compare people in your family. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
1

Warm up

Just talk. Full sentences this time.

  • Tell me about your family. Who's in it?
  • Who are you closest to? Why?
  • Who do you look like in your family?
  • Is there anyone in your family you really admire?
2

More family words

👨‍👩‍👧 Modern families

👆 Which of these do YOU have? Point and say them out loud.

3

What are they like?

Words to describe people. Tap a card to see it in a sentence.

🧍 How they look
😊 What they're like
🔑 look = appearance, be like = personality. "What does she look like?" → "She's tall." "What's she like?" → "She's really kind."
4

Talking about other people

For he / she / it, the verb gets an -s. Say each one out loud.

🔑 I work → she works. I live → he lives. I have → she has. Don't forget the -s!
5

Introduce your family

Build a real introduction, then say it out loud like you're introducing them to a friend. 👋

6

Compare your family

Use these to compare two people. Say three about your own family.

🔑 Short word + -er: tall → taller, old → older. Long word + more: more serious. And: than → "He's taller than me."
7

Keep talking

Pick a card and talk for 1 minute. No stopping.

8

You can do this ✓

Tap each one you can do now.

  • I can introduce a family member. This is my brother, Pedro.
  • I can say what they do. He works as a teacher.
  • I can describe people. She's tall and really kind.
  • I can compare. My sister is older than me.

Grammar reference
• 3rd person -s → she works, he lives, it has.
• look like vs be like → What does he look like? (appearance) · What's he like? (personality).
• Comparatives → taller than, more serious than.
• have got → She's got curly hair = She has curly hair.
